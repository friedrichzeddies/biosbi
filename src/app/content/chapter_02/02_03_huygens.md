### Huygens Principle

You should have noticed how minima and maxima form and how the phase influences this pattern. You can now see how the locations of the minima shift depending on the phase you select.

Now that two-source interference feels less mysterious, we can connect it to a deeper concept: the Huygens-Fresnel principle. The core idea is that any *wavefront* can be decomposed into many individual point sources. You gotten a little taste of it just now when you used two point sources to create a wavefront with a particular shape (e.g. changed the location of the minima).
