You should have spotted, how minima and maxima are forming and how phase influences this. You should have seen and now understand, how the locations of, e. g., the minima change depending on the phase you chose.

Now that two-source interference feels less mysterious, we can connect it to a deeper statement: the Huygens principle. The punchline is that a _wavefront_ can be decomposed into many little sources.

This abstract formulation can be easily simulated, and you are again invited to study how the wavefront behaves under changing parameters.
